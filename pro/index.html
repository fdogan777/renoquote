Active means Draft, Quoted, Approved or In Progress. Completed projects do not count. Creating or duplicating a project and reactivating a Completed project are checked against the limit; room and work-item duplication stay free.

Each successful new Free Customer Quotation generation consumes an export, including generating the same project again. Sharing an existing PDF, failed generation and Pro generation do not consume the Free allowance. The month uses UTC Gregorian boundaries; this ledger is local to the device, not synchronized across devices.

When Pro expires or is revoked, saved projects, customers, templates and company details are retained. Calculations, viewing and editing existing data remain available; future creation and premium export actions follow the Free policy.

Monthly and yearly options are auto-renewable subscriptions in one group. Lifetime is a one-time nonconsumable purchase. Lifetime does not itself cancel another subscription. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are available in the app. No commercial price or refund promise is made here.
