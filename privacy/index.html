Privacy Policy — draft

A behavior-based draft for the RenoQuote iOS app and this static website. No final effective date has been assigned.

Draft · owner approval required

These documents describe verified Build 22 behavior. They are not approved, final legal documents and must not be used to authorize production sales.

Local app information

The inspected Build 22 stores project and room measurements, work items, pricing, notes, customer information, templates and business information locally using SwiftData. Preferences, quotation-language/locale references, a logo-file reference and the monthly export ledger are local settings. The app does not implement a developer-operated account, backend, analytics or third-party AI API.

Logos, PDFs and sharing

Selecting a logo uses the native PhotosPicker for an image you choose; a normalized image is stored in Application Support, rather than large image data in preferences. PDFs can contain entered business, customer and project information and are generated as local temporary files. The native share sheet lets you choose a destination. A destination you select has its own data handling; this draft does not promise control over copies you share.

Apple services and purchases

StoreKit requests product information and uses Apple's verified transactions for access. Apple handles its own purchase/account services; RenoQuote does not provide a card-entry form. Build 22 production purchases are disabled. No claim is made here about Apple's full collection or retention practices; consult Apple's applicable notices. The app's usage ledger is separate from purchase entitlements.

This website and its hosting

This static site has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or contact form. Its JavaScript stores only an optional appearance preference in browser localStorage; it can be cleared through browser site-data controls. Language is selected through the URL. GitHub Pages hosting logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described by GitHub. Hosting behavior is distinct from this site's code; consult GitHub's privacy statement.

Your control and unresolved details

The app provides editing and deletion for projects, customers and templates, and logo removal. Deleting a customer preserves saved project/client information. Copies of exported PDFs, backups and files sent elsewhere are separate. Uninstalling, restoring and system backups may affect local data; retention for hosting, Apple services and any future support correspondence is not established by the app source. The owner must approve the operator identity, privacy contact, jurisdiction, legal basis/rights procedures, retention and any support-processing details before this becomes a final policy.
