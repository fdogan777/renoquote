Operator and scope

RenoQuote is operated by Fatih Doğan, an individual developer residing in Türkiye, not a registered company. The primary market is the United States; this alone does not determine every applicable privacy law. This policy covers the RenoQuote iOS app and https://fdogan777.github.io/renoquote/. Privacy and support contact: renoquote.support@gmail.com.

Information stored in the app

Projects, room dimensions, openings, work items, prices, financial assumptions, notes, customer records, work templates and business contact details are stored in the app container using SwiftData. Preferences, document-language/locale choices, the logo reference and PDF usage ledger are local settings. RenoQuote has no in-app user-account service, data-upload endpoint or app-managed cloud synchronization. Customer entries are not imported from your device address book.

Business logo and photo selection

PhotosPicker lets you select an image rather than granting RenoQuote a general view of your photo library. The app stores a resized PNG in Application Support and a filename reference in preferences. Removing a logo removes that stored file/reference; replacing it attempts to remove the old file. Apple may retrieve a selected image from your photo services. The developer does not receive the logo automatically.

PDFs and information you share

Quotations and reports are generated locally as temporary PDF files. Free Customer Quotations include entered company name, email, phone and address, but no company logo. Pro branding may include a logo. PDFs may also contain customer contacts, site information, notes and costs. Sharing is your explicit choice through the system share sheet. The selected app, storage service or recipient handles the shared copy under its own terms.

Apple analytics, diagnostics, StoreKit and local usage

For the initial release, we use only aggregate, non-identifiable App Sessions reports, App Crashes aggregate reports and Xcode Metrics Organizer summaries. Product Interaction serves Analytics; Crash Data and Performance Data serve App Functionality. These production categories are not linked to user identity and are not used for tracking. We do not combine these reports with identities, support correspondence or tester records, or rely on identifiable raw diagnostics for these declarations. Apple controls report availability and privacy safeguards. No third-party analytics, advertising SDK or tracking integration is included in the app. StoreKit obtains product information and verifies purchase access through Apple; there is no developer payment-card form or receipt-upload server. The successful Free PDF-generation counter stays on the device by UTC month; app code does not upload it.

TestFlight beta information

TestFlight beta feedback may identify testers and is separate from the production reports described above. Developer-downloaded or separately stored feedback, including associated screenshots and attachments, is deleted no later than 90 days after the related issue is resolved, except where lawful retention is required. This is handled through a manual procedure, not automatic deletion. Apple-hosted TestFlight records remain subject to Apple’s own retention practices; deleting a developer-held copy does not delete Apple’s copy.

Support correspondence

Emailing renoquote.support@gmail.com voluntarily discloses your sender address, message and any attachments to Fatih Doğan and the email providers, including Gmail. Support correspondence may identify you; it is separate from the three production-report categories above. We use it to understand and respond to your request, not for advertising or tracking. Send redacted examples rather than customer records or payment credentials. Support emails and related correspondence are retained for a maximum of 90 days after the support request is resolved. After that period, we delete these records unless retention is necessary for a valid legal obligation or another applicable lawful reason. This is a retention limit, not a promise of automatic deletion. Users may request deletion at renoquote.support@gmail.com; we respond to privacy requests within the deadlines required by applicable law.

Website hosting and preferences

The site has no added analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts or contact form. It stores an optional appearance preference in browser localStorage; language follows the URL. GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for hosting security. Hosting-provider processing is separate from website scripts. Browser settings can clear the appearance preference. The website does not automatically receive your local RenoQuote project database.

Deletion, retention and backups

Local records remain until you edit/delete them or the app’s storage is otherwise removed. You can edit/delete projects, rooms, work items, customers and templates, clear business fields and remove the logo. Deleting a customer directory record leaves contact snapshots in existing projects; edit/delete those separately. PDFs, recipient copies and device backups are separate and may remain. Cancelling Pro does not delete saved records. There is no fixed PDF cleanup schedule, guaranteed secure erasure or app-managed backup/recovery. Apple controls retention of records it hosts. Developer-held TestFlight feedback follows the 90-day rule above. Aggregate reports may not allow a particular person’s contribution to be isolated.

Privacy requests

For questions or requests concerning information held by the operator, email renoquote.support@gmail.com and identify the relevant correspondence or beta submission without sending unnecessary identity documents. Applicable law may give you rights of access, correction, deletion and complaint, which you can exercise through this address. Fatih Doğan cannot remotely erase a local project database that has never been received, or promise deletion of Apple-controlled records or recipient copies. Where needed, use Apple’s privacy/support tools or contact the recipient for their copies.
