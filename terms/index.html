Purpose and responsibility for estimates

RenoQuote calculates renovation estimates from your measurements, material specifications, waste, rates and pricing assumptions. Verify quantities, site conditions, manufacturer coverage, tax treatment and quotations before buying materials or agreeing work. Tile counts are estimates, especially for complex layouts. No quantity, cost or regulatory-compliance guarantee is offered; mandatory consumer rights are not excluded.

Free plan

Free includes calculations, customer management and all five languages. It permits three active projects and creation of up to three basic templates. Draft, Quoted, Approved and In Progress projects count as active; Completed projects do not. You may edit, delete and apply saved templates even above the creation limit. Existing data is not deleted merely because a limit is reached.

Free PDF allowance and Pro restrictions

Free permits five successful Customer Quotation generations per device per UTC calendar month. Regenerating a quotation counts again; sharing an existing file does not. Failed generation and blocked actions do not consume quota. Cancelling sharing after successful generation does not undo it. Free PDFs include company contacts without a logo. Logo branding and new Detailed Material Reports require Pro. The export screen shows usage, the next UTC reset and the denial reason.
