The product and its scope

RenoQuote is a native iOS 17+ renovation planning app with painting, flooring and tiling calculations, projects, customers, templates and PDF exports. Build 22 is the basis of this draft. No commercial release date, App Store availability or service-level commitment is established here.

Estimates and your inputs

Calculations use entered dimensions, openings, material coverage, waste and pricing. Verify measurements, layout, manufacturer specifications, labor assumptions and project requirements before purchase or contracting. Tile quantities are estimates. The draft makes no accuracy guarantee and does not replace professional site verification. Final liability wording requires jurisdiction-specific owner/legal review.

Free and Pro access

The Pro page describes current configurable Build 22 limits: three active projects, three Free Customer Quotation generations per UTC calendar month and up to three basic templates. Pro adds unlimited allowances and specified premium actions. Existing data is retained when entitlement ends. These policy details do not authorize production sales or establish final commercial terms.

Prepared purchase options

Monthly/yearly auto-renewable subscriptions and a lifetime nonconsumable are configured for development testing. Production purchases remain disabled. Actual prices must come from StoreKit in an available storefront; local test prices are not commercial offers. Lifetime does not automatically cancel another subscription. Restore and management are available in the app. Purchase, cancellation, renewal and refund terms must be reviewed with applicable Apple agreements and consumer rules; no independent refund promise is provided.

Your records and shared documents

You choose the information entered and destinations for exported documents. Check quotation totals, tax assumptions and customer details before sharing. Keep appropriate backups of local data, settings and logo files. There is no app-managed cloud sync in Build 22. Accountless local operation does not establish a guarantee against device loss or data loss.

Terms that still require approval

The operator/legal entity, contact, effective date, governing law, dispute procedure, user eligibility/age rules, license and intellectual-property terms, limitations of liability and mandatory consumer rights require owner/legal review. No registration details, address, legal guarantee or blanket waiver is invented in this draft. Do not treat it as a final agreement.
