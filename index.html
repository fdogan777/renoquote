Metric & imperial measurements

Every surface. A considered estimate.

Category-specific calculators turn room measurements into material quantities, with configurable waste and purchase units.

Painting

Measure walls and optional ceilings. Account for doors, windows, coats and coverage, then round purchases to whole paint containers.

Flooring

Subtract excluded areas, add your waste allowance and calculate the whole packages needed using coverage and package price.

Tiling

Estimate tile quantities with grout spacing, waste, tiles per box and box pricing. Complex layouts still require on-site verification.

One renovation workspace

Keep the work connected.

From a single room to a multi-room project, keep measurements, customers, work items and pricing together. Adjust inputs before explicitly saving your changes.

Projects & customers

Manage rooms and multiple work items, organize customer records and link customers to projects.

Pricing you control

Configure material prices, labor rates, overhead, profit markup, discounts and taxes. Each project's currency stays independent.

Two purposeful PDF formats

Send a concise Customer Quotation or export a Detailed Material Report with technical calculations. Detailed reports and company branding require Pro.

Reusable work templates

Apply independent snapshots of material and labor settings without linking existing work to later template changes.

Your workspace. Your language.

English, Turkish, Spanish, German and French are included in both plans. Quotation language and currency formatting are configured separately.
